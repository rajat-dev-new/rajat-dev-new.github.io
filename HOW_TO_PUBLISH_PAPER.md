## Option 1: Zenodo (CERN / OpenAIRE) — ⭐ Recommended (Takes 5 Minutes & Instant DOI)

**Zenodo** was built by CERN (the European Organization for Nuclear Research) under the EU OpenAIRE program. It is free, open to all researchers and engineers, and issues a permanent, citable **DOI** immediately upon submission. It is automatically indexed by **Google Scholar**, **ResearchGate**, and university libraries worldwide.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open **[https://zenodo.org](https://zenodo.org)** in your browser.
2. Click **Log In** in the top-right corner.
   - *Tip:* Select **"Log in with GitHub"** to authenticate instantly with your `rajat-dev-new` GitHub account.
3. Once logged in, click **"New upload"** (or go to `https://zenodo.org/deposit/new`).
4. **Files:** Drag and drop `Deterministic_Multi_Agent_Systems_Rajat_Patle.pdf` from your `Documents/RAJAT_int` folder.
5. **Basic Information:**
   - **Resource type:** Select **Publication** &rarr; **Preprint** (or *Working Paper*).
   - **Title:** `Deterministic Multi-Agent Systems in Production: Architecture, State Machine Guardrails, and Failure Modes`
   - **Authors:** 
     - Family name: `Patle`
     - Given names: `Rajat`
     - Affiliation: `NTT DATA Information Processing Services`
     - ORCID: *(leave blank if you don't have one yet, or create one in 1 min at orcid.org)*
   - **Description:** Copy and paste the Abstract from the paper.
   - **License:** Select **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** (default open access).
   - **Keywords:** `Multi-Agent Systems, LangGraph, Deterministic Software Engineering, LLM Guardrails, Credit Risk, Cloud Architecture, Python, AWS`

## Option 2: TechRxiv (by IEEE) — High Engineering Prestige

**TechRxiv** is the official open-access preprint server operated by **IEEE** (the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) for computer science, software engineering, and electrical systems.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to **[https://www.techrxiv.org](https://www.techrxiv.org)**.
2. Create a free account or log in with your IEEE credentials.
3. Click **"Submit a Preprint"**.
4. Upload `Deterministic_Multi_Agent_Systems_Rajat_Patle.pdf`.
5. Enter title, abstract, and author details (same as above).
6. Select subject area: **Computing and Processing** / **Artificial Intelligence** / **Software Engineering**.
7. Submit! TechRxiv staff conducts a brief moderation check (usually 2–3 business days) and issues an official IEEE TechRxiv DOI.
